How to write the plan
  • Day 1 | Nov 22 · Hanoi | Hanoi — day number, then the date line, then the title
  • [On arrival · coordinated with flight] — square brackets become the small label above a card
  • HAN Airport Pick-Up | 2100 — a title, then its cost after a pipe
  • The next plain line becomes the description
  • - Private transfer vehicle — dashes become the bullet list
  • = INR 2,318 × 5 adults — an equals sign makes the small grey cost note

Sections: start a line with Hotels, Pricing, Inclusions, Exclusions or Notes and list beneath it.

Hotels: Hanoi | Sky Lark Hotel | Nov 22 – 24 | 2 nights | 21533, then dashes for room types.

Pricing: Margin | 45000, GST | 5%, TCS | 2%. Day totals, base cost, subtotal, GST/TCS and the grand total are all worked out for you, with tax shown as its own section.

Children: mention 2 children or 2 kids anywhere in the opening lines and it appears as its own stat, and counts toward cost per person.

Terms: start a line with Terms or Cancellation Policy and list beneath it.

Trip summary: every entry is sorted into Morning, Afternoon or Evening automatically — from words like "evening" or a time like 15:30. On the Trip summary page, click any entry to move it to a different column.

Every card gets a photo slot. Click it, or drag an image straight onto it.
